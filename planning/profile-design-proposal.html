User Profile Page — “Social Rail” redesign

A redesign of our profile page that combines a social-media feel with a full-width, side-by-side layout. A sticky identity card on the left holds the avatar, stats, and skills; the right side is a scrollable feed of the user's experiences. The goal: match our platform's social theme and remove the empty white space in the current design.

SideHustle · Team Power Panel — Zainab, Ariane, Ardelia · For team design review

👀 Teammates — what to look at

  • Layout: Does the left rail + right feed feel balanced and fill the width well?
  • Social feel: Do the experience "posts" match the vibe we want for the platform?
  • Stats & skills: Are Listings / Reviews / Rating and the skills chips in the right place?
  • Anything missing? e.g. resume/certification links, applications tab, "edit profile".
