Profile Listing Image — Layout Options

Three ways to show images on listing cards without the ugly zoom problem.

Option A
Thumbnail on the right
Keep the card compact. The image sits as a small rounded thumbnail on the right side — like a preview. The card height stays consistent whether there's an image or not.

Option B
Slim banner on top
A short wide banner above the card content — much shorter than the home feed (96px vs 200px), so the zoom is less extreme. Cards with no image skip the banner entirely.

Option C
Square thumbnail on the left
The image is a fixed square on the left edge of the card, stretching the full card height. Content sits to its right. No zoom issue — the square just crops to fill. Cards without images keep the same layout (avatar fills the left side).
