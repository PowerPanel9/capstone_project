The Problem

Finding freelance work shouldn't be this hard

Local service marketplaces leave both sides frustrated — mismatched expectations, manual searching, and zero intelligent discovery.

🔍

No Smart Matching

Clients manually scroll through listings with no guidance on which providers fit their needs.

📋

Listing Creation is Painful

Writing a clear job listing from scratch takes time — most clients don't know what details to include.

💸

Payments are Fragmented

Handling payment and job completion across platforms leads to disputes and lack of accountability.

03 / 08
The Solution

SideHustle — your intelligent
local freelance platform

A full-stack marketplace where AI does the heavy lifting — from creating listings to ranking the best applicants.

🤖

AI Chatbot

Create listings, search providers, and get matched — all through a natural conversation.

⚡

Smart Matching

AI ranks applicants by skills, location, and reviews so clients can accept with confidence.

💳

Integrated Payments

Pay providers directly through the platform. Jobs are marked complete when both sides agree.
